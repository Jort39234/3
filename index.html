The Industrial Revolution and Global Urbanization (1760–1840)

The Industrial Revolution marked a profound turning point in human history, transitioning agrarian economies into industrialized urban centers powered by mechanical innovation. The mechanization of the textile industries and the development of the Watt steam engine exponentially expanded productivity while fundamentally reshaping societal hierarchies.

Primary areas of study for upcoming examination:

  • Technological innovations in metallurgical processing (coke-fueled blast furnaces).
  • Demographic migration from rural commons into factory towns (Manchester, Birmingham).
  • Public health impacts, water distribution infrastructure, and the Factory Acts of 1833.
